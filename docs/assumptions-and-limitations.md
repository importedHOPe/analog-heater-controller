# Assumptions and limitations

- Water is represented by one uniform temperature state.
- Water mass and specific heat are treated as constant.
- Heat input is proportional to heater state and assumed transfer efficiency.
- Heat loss is approximated by one overall coefficient, `UA`.
- The current model does not represent spatial temperature gradients, vessel-wall thermal storage, changing properties, evaporation, boiling, or dry heating.
- The screenshots do not establish all parameter values or solver settings.

Label results as simulated and record parameters, initial conditions, solver, step settings, and stop time. A continuous-ON comparison illustrates only that specific scenario; it is not a universal energy-saving claim or necessarily an equivalent-service comparison.

Recommended sequence: document `UA`; perform sensitivity analysis; add continuous-ON scenario using the same plant; then consider wall dynamics and boiling/evaporation.
